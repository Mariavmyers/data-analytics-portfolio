## How I Approach Analysis

Each project is built around a business decision, not just a dashboard. My process is:

1. Define the business problem and decision.
2. Inspect and clean the data.
3. Define the relevant KPIs.
4. Analyze patterns and exceptions.
5. Build clear visualizations or dashboards.
6. Translate findings into recommendations.
7. Document assumptions, limitations, and methodology.

## Data Ethics

Portfolio projects may use synthetic or public datasets. Synthetic projects are clearly labeled. No confidential employer data, protected health information, customer information, or proprietary business data is used.
